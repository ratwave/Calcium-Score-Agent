## Emerging plaque-relief/removal research watchlist

No routine clinical therapy currently "scrapes out" coronary plaque; current care focuses on stabilization, event reduction, and in some settings modest regression.

- **Lp(a)HORIZON (pelacarsen)** (Lipoprotein(a) lowering): Tests whether major Lp(a) reduction lowers hard CV outcomes.
- **OCEAN(a)-Outcomes (olpasiran)** (Lipoprotein(a) lowering (siRNA)): Large outcomes trial for RNA-based Lp(a) lowering.
- **PCSK9-pathway intensification programs** (Extreme LDL-C reduction): Imaging evidence supports plaque burden/composition improvement with very low LDL-C.
- **Colchicine and inflammation-targeted secondary prevention** (Residual inflammatory risk): Some trials show event reduction in chronic coronary populations; selection and tolerability remain key.

## Notes for discussion with a clinician

- Are LDL-C targets/intensity adequate for very high risk status?
- Is additional lipid therapy (ezetimibe/PCSK9-pathway/inclisiran) appropriate?
- Is elevated Lp(a) present and actionable in trial-era management?
- Is inflammation-focused prevention (e.g., colchicine in selected patients) appropriate?
- Are blood pressure, glycemia, sleep apnea, and exercise prescriptions optimized?
